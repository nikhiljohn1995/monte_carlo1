A+B is the only world where A is free. When the guard says "B", A+B shows up twice as often as B+C, because in A+B he is forced to say B, while in B+C he says B only half the time. Ignore the statement and just ask whether B is released, and the two worlds are equally likely, which gives the 1/2 from the original (flawed) argument.
